Key Insights
Delivery rate improved significantly. This period averaged 4.2% failure vs 10.5% in May. Cleaned lists are making a clear difference.
Retail & Commercial Leasing NSW was the top revenue campaign at $3,190 from 7 registrations despite mismatches. Conveyancing and leasing audiences responded well.
Message mismatches still happening. Several contacts received the wrong SMS but still converted. If the right message went to the right audience the conversion would likely be higher.
Psychosocial NON LAW sent to 11,100 people and drove 4 registrations at $640 total. Low revenue per recipient at $0.06, but the volume is large.
Succession All States drove $1,230 from 3 registrations despite being a wills/estate focused send. Good topic match for estate lawyers.
No Expired failures this period. Only Failed (63%) and Rejected (37%), no expired. This suggests the timing of sends was better and phones were reachable.
